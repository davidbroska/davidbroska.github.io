Bio

I am a PhD student in the Department of Sociology at Stanford University. I joined Stanford after receiving an MSc in Social Research Methods from the London School of Economics and completing a BA in Sociology, Politics, and Economics at Zeppelin University in Germany.

One line of my work examines how generative AI can contribute to empirical social science. A second line of work studies public attitudes toward economic policy.

I am on the job market in 2026–2027.
